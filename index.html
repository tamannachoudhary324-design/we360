Spendesk is a spend management platform for finance teams. It connects every type of company spend, from corporate cards and expenses to accounts payable, procurement, and budgets, into one system. Finance teams use Spendesk to get complete visibility, automate workflows, and close faster.

Spendesk is ideal for companies from 50 employees to 250, but we do support businesses under or above these numbers. Whether you’re scaling fast, managing multiple entities, or dealing with complex spend operations, the platform grows with you. If you’ve outgrown basic tools but want to avoid enterprise complexity, Spendesk is built for that space.

Most customers are fully live within 3-4 weeks, including data migration, policy setup, and team onboarding. We provide hands-on implementation support to help you launch fast without disrupting day-to-day operations.

For most customers, yes. Spendesk typically replaces multiple point solutions (corporate cards, expense management, AP software, procurement tools) with one connected platform. You’ll get broader functionality, better visibility, and simpler operations without managing multiple vendors.
